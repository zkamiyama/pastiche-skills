# Original-writing and coherence checklist

Plan each scene with fields: `scene`, `protagonist_goal`, `belief_before`, `event`, `decision`, `consequence`, `belief_after`, `reader_knows`, `unresolved_question`.

During drafting: preserve the user's specified viewpoint and register; do not borrow named entities or story beats from the corpus. During revision, check that knowledge is acquired before a character acts upon it, injuries/distances/timing are consistent, reversals have planted causes, and each scene advances an objective.

Use the Style Bible as **soft ranges**, not an author-impersonation guarantee. Check narrative coherence and content preservation separately from style. Where a requested genre conflicts with a surface metric, preserve readability and narrative causality.

For long texts, process in stages: outline → scene blocks with state notes → continuity revision → style pass. This operationalizes findings from Re3 (Yang et al., EMNLP 2022), DOME (Wang et al., NAACL 2025), and STORYTELLER (Li et al., ACL 2025) without claiming to reproduce their algorithms.
